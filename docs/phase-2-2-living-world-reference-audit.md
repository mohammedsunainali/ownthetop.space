# Phase 2.2 living-world reference audit

This is an interaction audit, not an asset or implementation source. The approved local OwnTheTop tower and V2 facade remain the visual source of truth.

| Reference | What works | Appropriate for OwnTheTop | Do not copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [AI Traffic in Bengaluru](https://blrai.vercel.app/) | The base page was unavailable during this audit, and no signed URL was supplied in this turn; no specific behavior is asserted. | Retain only the task's general principle that traffic should visibly follow its road network. | Any traffic map, data, vehicles, signed URL, or token. |
| [Token Town](https://sael.net/token-town/) | A small district uses purposeful crowds and routes to make quantitative information feel spatial. Its opening state and time controls make the scene legible. | Keep a bounded, miniature district with movement tied to actual paths and restrained first-load staging. | Shops, data model, timelapse effects, slogans, typography, and art. |
| [Model Museum](https://sael.net/ai-museum/) | A guided tour and selectable timeline make a complex 3D space approachable. | Borrow the principle of a brief, cancellable guided entry that yields to manual input. | Rooms, artifacts, historical content, museum layout, and tour UI. |
| [Apple Park](https://sael.net/apple-park/) | Landscape, roads, paths, and trees form a coherent campus; multiple views clarify scale. | Arrange existing props into deliberate plaza, green, mobility, and outer-district layers. | Geographic map, lidar-based geometry, ring building, trees, roads, lighting design, or camera implementation. |
| [TopFloor.Company](https://topfloor.company/) | The public page establishes a clear tower-led competition and compact framing. The user-provided screenshots show a world-first arrival. | Use an original OwnTheTop loading message and keep the leaderboard towers the focal point. | Source, imagery, branding, copy, model, soundtrack, or literal layout. |

The reference sites are not used at runtime. All district geometry and motion are local procedural extensions of the existing OwnTheTop scene.
