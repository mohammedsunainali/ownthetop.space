# OwnTheTop Mascot Guidelines

The Skyline / Summit mascot is locked.

Preserve: rounded light body, deep navy visor, simple eyes, blue architectural peaks, central gold summit, rounded blue side appendages and small feet. The gold summit always signifies the top.

## 3D
Canonical source: Higgsfield 3D Jutsu project `d106d253-ecae-43de-933d-946c322d6c53`, revision 3. Poses must derive from the same master geometry/material hierarchy.

## 2D
The flat SVG is an intentional vector interpretation for small UI/editorial use. It must not be presented as the 3D master.

## Poses
Default, Flying Up, Celebration, Waving, Happy, Sitting, Thinking, Surprised, Outbid and Victory #1.

## Never
No animal, rocket, bird, crown, human, replacement robot, generic blob, photorealism, chrome or cyberpunk treatment.
