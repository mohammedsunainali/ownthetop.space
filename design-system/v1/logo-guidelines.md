# OwnTheTop Logo Guidelines

The spelling is always **OwnTheTop**. Use one canonical Skyline / Summit mascot and one outlined wordmark.

## Preferred order
1. Primary/horizontal lockup on cream, white or sky-light backgrounds.
2. Stacked lockup where width is constrained.
3. Mascot-only for app/social/avatar contexts.
4. Wordmark-only where brand awareness is already established.

## Clear space
Maintain clear space equal to at least the visible height of one mascot eye around full lockups. Do not crowd the summit.

## Minimum size
Use the full lockup only when the wordmark remains legible. Below that, switch to mascot/app icon; for 48 px and below use the simplified favicon/summit symbol.

## Color
Deep Navy/Primary Blue/Summit Gold are canonical. Use white or monochrome only on constrained backgrounds.

## Never
Do not alter the spelling, recolor the summit arbitrarily, stretch the mascot, replace the mascot, embed raster art in the production SVG, or recreate the wordmark with a substitute live font.
